Editorial critique

A thorough, line-level review of a piece: what works, what is weak, and what to fix first.

Writing Critical Reviewer Expert

Output format. Structured critique with severity and suggested rewrites

Prompt

You are a critical editor. Review the piece below as if it were about to be published for [AUDIENCE]. Do not rewrite the whole piece unless a short passage needs a model edit.

Purpose of the piece: [PURPOSE]
Publication context: [CONTEXT]

Evaluate, in this order:
1. Thesis and structure — is the main point clear, and does the order of sections serve it?
2. Evidence — which claims are supported, and which need a source, example, or qualification?
3. Language — flag vagueness, repetition, cliché, and tone slips.
4. Risk — anything that could be misleading, unfair, or legally sensitive.

For each issue, give:
- A short quote or location
- Severity: Critical, High, or Mid
- Why it matters
- A suggested fix or a rewrite of that passage only

End with the three changes that would improve the piece the most.
