For organizations

Enterprise

Promptly for teams that already use Microsoft 365. People sign in with work accounts. Prompts stay on the Mac or in your SharePoint — we don’t host your library.

  • Sign in with Microsoft Entra ID Work or school accounts, through the official Microsoft sign-in on the Mac. Tokens stay in the Mac Keychain.
  • Admin console for seats A separate admin surface for licences and the Promptly.Admin role — not a redesign of the Mac app. Consumer sign-in stays as it is today.
  • SharePoint as the team library Your organization grants Promptly access to the sites you choose. Library content is treated as untrusted input, the same way selected text is.
  • Optional Microsoft Purview DLP Before text is sent to an AI service, Promptly can ask Purview to check it. Purview can block, warn, or audit — it cannot mask text. If Purview is unavailable, Promptly does not send. Checked text is processed in your Microsoft tenant, not on our servers.
  • Usage analytics you can turn off Analytics is off until someone opts in, and never includes prompt bodies or selected text. Organizations can disable it (including via MDM).
