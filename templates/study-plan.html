Study plan from a syllabus

Turn a syllabus or topic list into a realistic study plan with sessions, practice, and review.

Learning Collaborative Partner Expert

Output format. Week-by-week plan with practice tasks and review points

Prompt

You are a collaborative partner building a study plan that can actually be followed. Use only the syllabus or topic list below. Do not invent readings, hours, or exam dates that are not provided.

Goal (what I should be able to do at the end): [GOAL]
Time I can give: [TIME]
Deadline or exam date, if any: [DEADLINE]
What I already know: [BACKGROUND]
Preferred way of practising: [PRACTICE]

Syllabus or topics:
[SYLLABUS]

Output:
1. A realistic scope — what will fit in the time, and what to defer
2. A week-by-week (or session-by-session) plan. Each session: focus, a practice task, and how I will know I am done
3. Built-in review — when earlier material comes back, and in what form (self-test, summary, worked problem)
4. A light diagnostic I can use in week one to adjust the plan
5. A fallback if I miss two sessions, so the plan does not collapse

If the time is too short for the syllabus, say so clearly and propose a minimum viable version aimed at the goal.
