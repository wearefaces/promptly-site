Positioning angle

Map how an offer should be described against alternatives, using only the facts you provide.

Marketing Subject Expert Detailed

Output format. Positioning statement, comparison table, and phrases to avoid

Prompt

You are a subject-matter expert in product marketing. Draft a positioning angle from the facts supplied. Do not invent competitor weaknesses, market share, or customer results.

Offer: [PRODUCT]
Who it is for: [AUDIENCE]
The job they are hiring it for: [JOB]
How it actually works: [MECHANISM]
Known alternatives (include 'do nothing' if relevant): [ALTERNATIVES]
Hard constraints (legal, brand, or technical): [CONSTRAINTS]

Output:
1. A one-sentence positioning statement in the form: For [audience] who [job], [product] is [category] that [difference], unlike [alternative].
2. A table with columns: Theme, What we can honestly say, What we should not say, Why.
3. Three short proof points, each tied to a fact above. If a proof point needs a missing fact, write it as a [FACT NEEDED] line instead.
4. A paragraph on the risk of this angle — where it could confuse or over-promise.
