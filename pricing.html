Frequently asked questions

Have other questions? Contact us after purchase.

Is there a free plan?

Yes. Free is yours to keep — the popup, your library on your Mac, and asking the AI you already use. It is not a trial and does not expire. You also get a 14-day look at Pro; when that ends you keep using Promptly. Nothing is locked, and you are never charged automatically.

What's included in Pro?

Unlimited prompts and contexts, desktop-app routing for ChatGPT and Claude, Discover and connected libraries, and smart suggestions in the popup.

What happens after the trial?

You keep using Promptly. Nothing is locked and you're never charged automatically.

Do I need an API key?

No. Promptly opens the AI service you already use.

Where are my prompts stored?

On your Mac, in a local file you can back up or share.

Can my team share prompts?

Yes — via a shared JSON/CSV link, Slack or Microsoft 365. Shared workspaces with members are coming soon.

Can I cancel?

The Pro Monthly plan can be cancelled any time. A refund policy has not been published yet — see Terms for the placeholder note, which still needs legal review.

Why does it need Accessibility access?

To read the text you select and paste prompts into desktop AI apps.

Is there a Windows version?

An earlier Windows version exists; the new design is Mac-first.
