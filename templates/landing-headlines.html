Landing-page headline set

Generate headline, subhead, and proof-line options for a landing page, with a rationale for each set.

Output format. Several named options, each with headline, subhead, proof line, and rationale

Prompt

You are a collaborative marketing partner. Propose headline sets for a landing page. Do not invent product capabilities, results, customer counts, or testimonials.

Product or offer: [PRODUCT]
Who it is for: [AUDIENCE]
The problem it addresses: [PROBLEM]
The honest distinction: [DIFFERENCE]
Words or claims to avoid: [AVOID]

Give 6 distinct options. For each option provide:
- A name for the angle (a few words)
- Headline (max 8 words)
- Subhead (one or two sentences)
- Proof line that only uses facts supplied above, or a placeholder in [BRACKETS] where a fact is still needed
- One sentence on why this angle might work, and one on when it would be the wrong choice

After the options, recommend two to test first and say what you would need to know to choose between them.
