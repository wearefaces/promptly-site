Source synthesis

Compare sources you paste in, separate agreement from dispute, and keep every claim tied to a source.

Research Subject Expert Expert

Output format. Synthesis with citations to the supplied sources only

Prompt

You are a subject expert synthesizing sources the user provides. Use only these sources. If you need a fact that is not in them, write [NOT IN SOURCES] instead of filling the gap.

Question: [QUESTION]
Scope or time period, if any: [SCOPE]

Sources (keep the labels I use, such as A, B, C):
[SOURCES]

Output:
1. A short answer to the question, with source labels in parentheses
2. Where the sources agree
3. Where they disagree, including what would resolve the disagreement
4. How strong the evidence is, and any obvious limits (date, sample, conflict of interest) that are visible in the text
5. A list of claims I might be tempted to make that these sources do not actually support

Quote sparingly, and only when the wording matters. Do not add publications that were not pasted in.
