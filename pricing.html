Frequently asked questions

Have other questions? Contact us after purchase.

Is there a free plan?

Yes. Free is yours to keep — the popup, a library of up to 20 prompts and contexts on your Mac or Windows PC, and asking the AI you already use. It is not a trial and does not expire. Everyone also gets a 14-day Pro trial, no card needed; when that ends you keep using Free.

What's included in Pro?

Unlimited prompts and contexts, desktop-app routing for ChatGPT and Claude, Discover and connected libraries, and smart suggestions in the popup.

What happens after the trial?

The trial never charges you. When it ends, Promptly continues on the Free plan. If you subscribe, Monthly and Yearly plans renew until cancelled. Lifetime is a one-time purchase.

Do I need an API key?

No. Promptly opens the AI service you already use.

Where are my prompts stored?

On your Mac or Windows PC, in a local file you can back up or share.

Can my team share prompts?

Yes — via a shared JSON/CSV link, Slack or Microsoft 365. Shared workspaces with members are coming soon.

Can I cancel?

The Pro Monthly and Yearly plans renew until cancelled. Lifetime is a one-time purchase. A refund policy has not been published yet — see Terms for the placeholder note, which still needs legal review.

Why does it need Accessibility access?

To read the text you select and paste prompts into desktop AI apps.

Is there a Windows version?

Windows is coming. Join the waitlist to hear when it is ready.
