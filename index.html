How it works

Three steps, from any app on your Mac.

1

Select text

In any app — browser, Mail, Slack, Word, or a code editor.

2

Pick a prompt

The Promptly popup appears next to your cursor, with your library one click away.

3

Ask your AI

Promptly opens ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot or another service with the prompt and your text already filled in.

Built for the Mac you already use.

Promptly is a menu bar app — no Dock icon. Your library stays on your Mac.

  • Stored locally

    Your library is stored on your Mac, in a local file you can back up or share.

  • Sign-in, library stays local

    You sign in with email, Google, or Apple. Your prompts stay on your Mac — we don’t upload your library.

  • You choose when text is sent

    Promptly sends text only to the AI service you choose, when you click Ask.

  • Tokens stay in Keychain

    Workspace tokens for Slack and Microsoft 365 are kept in the macOS Keychain.

  • On-device quality checks

    AI quality checks can run fully on-device with Apple Intelligence.

A popup, a library, and a path to the AI you already use.

The popup

Appears next to your cursor when you select text. Choose Full or Minimal, and switch any time. It never covers the selection, and flips at the screen edges.

Send to any AI

If a supported desktop app is installed, Promptly opens it and pastes automatically. Otherwise it opens the browser, or copies to the clipboard.

Prompt and context library

Save prompts and contexts, organise them with categories, and star favorites so they show first in the popup.

Prompt quality score

An instant 0–100 score with a colour indicator, plus Check and Improve with AI. Runs on-device when Apple Intelligence is on. The instant score currently understands English prompts best.

Discover prompts

Browse connected libraries such as prompts.chat. Copy, Ask, or Save a prompt into a category in one click.

Questions

Does it work in every app?

It works wherever you can select text. Most apps expose the selection directly; in others Promptly briefly uses copy to read it.

Which AI does it use?

The one you choose: ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok, DeepSeek, Mistral, Perplexity, Copilot, or your own URL.

Do I need an API key?

No. Promptly opens the AI service you already use.

Where are my prompts stored?

On your Mac, in a local file you can back up or share.

Can my team share prompts?

Yes — via a shared JSON/CSV link, Slack or Microsoft 365.

Why does it need Accessibility access?

To read the text you select and paste prompts into desktop AI apps.

What happens after the trial?

It's a 14-day free trial, cancel anytime. Cancel before the trial ends if you don't want to continue on the plan you chose.

Is there a Windows version?

An earlier Windows version exists; the new design is Mac-first.
