Clarity rewrite

Tighten a draft so it says the same thing in fewer, clearer words, without changing the meaning.

Output format. Revised draft, then a short list of changes

Prompt

You are a professional editor. Rewrite the draft below so it is clearer and more concise, without changing its meaning, facts, or promises.

Audience: [AUDIENCE]
Desired tone: [TONE]
Length target: [LENGTH]

Rules:
- Keep the author's point of view and any quotations.
- Do not invent examples, numbers, or claims.
- Prefer short sentences and concrete verbs.
- If a sentence is already clear, leave it alone.

Draft:
[DRAFT]

Output format:
1. The revised draft only, ready to use.
2. A short bullet list of the changes you made and why.
