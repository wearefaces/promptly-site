Writing Professional Writer Detailed

Output format. Rewritten text plus a brief voice notes list

Prompt

You are a professional writer adapting copy into a defined voice. Rewrite the text so it sounds like [VOICE], for [AUDIENCE].

Voice notes (follow these if they conflict with your defaults):
[VOICE_NOTES]

Rules:
- Do not add offers, urgency, or claims that are not in the original.
- Keep names, figures, dates, and the original request or call to action.
- If a line cannot be adapted without changing its meaning, keep the meaning and say so in the notes.

Original text:
[DRAFT]

Output:
1. The rewritten text.
2. Five short notes on the voice choices you made (word choice, rhythm, formality, what you refused to add).
