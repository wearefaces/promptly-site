Campaign brief builder

Turn scattered notes into a clear campaign brief, and list the questions still unanswered.

Marketing Collaborative Partner Expert

Output format. Brief with open questions called out in brackets

Prompt

You are a collaborative partner helping to write a campaign brief. Use only the notes below. If something important is missing, put a [QUESTION] in place — do not invent a target, budget, metric, or promise.

Campaign working title: [TITLE]
Notes:
[NOTES]

Write a brief with these sections:
1. Context — what is changing and why this campaign exists now
2. Audience — who we are speaking to, and who we are not
3. Job to be done — the action we want, in one sentence
4. Message — the single idea the work should leave behind
5. Offers and constraints — what we can say, show, or give; what we must not
6. Channels and assets — a practical list, marked as confirmed or [TO CONFIRM]
7. Success — how we will know it worked, only if the notes include a measure; otherwise list [QUESTION]s
8. Risks and dependencies
9. Open questions — a numbered list for the team

Keep the language plain enough that a designer, a media buyer, and a lawyer could all use the same document.
