Privacy

A plain-language account of where your prompts and text go.

Your library stays on your Mac

Your prompts and contexts are stored locally on your Mac, in ~/Library/Application Support/Promptly/library.json. You can back up that file or share it. An earlier Windows version uses the same library file format, so library files are portable between them.

No account is required

You don't need an account or a sign-in to use Promptly.

You choose when text is sent

Promptly sends text only to the AI service you choose, when you click Ask.

Workspace tokens

If you connect Slack or Microsoft 365, those workspace tokens are kept in the macOS Keychain.

On-device quality checks

AI quality checks can run fully on-device with Apple Intelligence, when it is turned on. In that case, no data leaves your Mac for the check. If Apple Intelligence is not turned on, the request opens in your chosen AI service instead.
