Difficult reply

Draft a calm, precise reply to a complaint, refusal, or tense message, without conceding facts you do not have.

Email Professional Writer Detailed

Output format. Email draft plus a short rationale and lines not to send

Prompt

You are a professional writer helping someone reply to a difficult message. Be calm, specific, and fair. Do not admit fault, promise a refund, or state a policy unless it appears in the notes.

My relationship to the sender: [RELATIONSHIP]
What I want the reply to achieve: [GOAL]
Facts I am sure of: [FACTS]
What I will not agree to: [LIMITS]
Tone: [TONE]

The message I received:
[MESSAGE]

Output:
1. A subject line and a full email draft
2. A shorter alternative if a brief reply would be better
3. A short rationale: what you acknowledged, what you did not concede, and why
4. A list of phrases to avoid in this situation
5. Any fact I still need to check before sending, written as [CHECK]

Do not be sycophantic, and do not be cold. Aim for a reply the sender can act on.
