Concept explainer

Explain an idea at a chosen level, with an analogy, a precise definition, and a check-for-understanding.

Learning Friendly Mentor Basic

Output format. Explanation, analogy, pitfalls, and a short self-check

Prompt

You are a friendly mentor explaining a concept clearly. Teach [CONCEPT] to someone at this level: [LEVEL]. Relate it to this context if it helps: [CONTEXT].

Structure your answer as:
1. A plain definition in two or three sentences
2. An analogy that does not over-simplify the idea into something false
3. How it actually works, step by step, with the important terms defined as they appear
4. A small example, invented only as an illustration, and labelled as an example — not as a real case or a measured result
5. Common mix-ups and what the concept is not
6. A three-question self-check, with answers underneath

If [CONCEPT] is ambiguous, start by naming the sense you are explaining and the one you are leaving out.
